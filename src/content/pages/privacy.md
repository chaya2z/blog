title: Privacy Policy
updatedAt: 2026-10-15
---

This site does not ask for your name, email address, or other contact details, and sets no
analytics or advertising cookies.

The site is hosted on Cloudflare and uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand how the site is
used and how it performs. Web Analytics records things like the page viewed, the referring site,
your country, browser and device type, and page load times. According to Cloudflare, it does not
use cookies or fingerprinting and does not track you across sites.

Cloudflare also processes request data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site, and
may set cookies that are strictly necessary for security. See
[Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).
